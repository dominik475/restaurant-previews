Common Questions

Painting FAQs

How do you prepare surfaces before painting?
Proper prep is everything. We clean, sand, fill holes and cracks, caulk gaps, and prime surfaces before any paint goes on. That's what makes our finishes last.
Do you offer color consultations?
Yes — we'll help you choose colors that work for your space and lighting. Bring your ideas and we'll make sure you love the result before we start.
How long does interior painting take?
A typical room takes 1–2 days. Whole-home interiors usually take 3–5 days depending on size and prep work needed. We work efficiently without sacrificing quality.
Are your painters licensed and insured?
Yes — fully licensed and insured. We protect your home and our crew on every job.
