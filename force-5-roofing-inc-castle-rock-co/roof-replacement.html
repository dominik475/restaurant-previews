When It’s Time for a New Roof

Signs You Need Replacement

Colorado's hail seasons, UV exposure, and heavy snow loads accelerate roof aging. If your roof is 15+ years old or has sustained storm damage, replacement is often more cost-effective than repeated repairs.

  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
  • Granule loss in gutters after storms
  • Interior leaks or water stains on ceilings
  • Roof 15+ years old with hail history
  • Insurance adjuster recommends replacement
  • Sagging deck or structural damage

Common Questions

Roof Replacement FAQs

How much does roof replacement cost in Denver?
Most residential replacements in Denver range from $8,000–$20,000 depending on size, pitch, and materials. Many customers pay $0–$500 out of pocket after insurance covers storm damage. We provide free estimates.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most homes are completed in 1–2 days. Larger or more complex roofs may take 3 days. We work efficiently and never leave your home exposed overnight.
Will my insurance cover roof replacement?
If your roof has hail or wind damage, your homeowner's insurance very likely covers full replacement. We're insurance claim specialists and will help document the damage and negotiate with your adjuster.
Do I need to be home during the replacement?
You don't need to be home during installation, but we recommend being present for the final walkthrough so we can review the completed work and hand over your warranty documents in person.
What class of shingles should I get?
For any Colorado home, we recommend Class 4 impact-resistant shingles. They survive 2-inch hail, qualify for insurance discounts of 10–30%, and typically pay back the cost premium within 5–7 years through savings.
