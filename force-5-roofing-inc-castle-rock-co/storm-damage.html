How It Works

The Insurance Claims Process

Step by step, from storm to installed roof — typically 30–60 days.

1

Free Inspection First

Call us before your insurance company. We document all damage professionally so you enter the process with evidence — not just a claim number.

2

File the Claim

Once you have documentation, we help you file. You’ll need your policy number, date of loss, and description of damage. They assign a claim number and schedule an adjuster.

3

Adjuster Inspection

This is critical. We attend the adjuster meeting with you. Our contractor identifies all damage and advocates for proper scope — adjusters often miss items.

4

Review & Supplement

We review the scope of loss with you. If items are missing, we submit supplements — documented requests for additional coverage. This is normal and expected.

5

Installation

You sign, we schedule. Your insurance pays us directly minus your deductible. Your only out-of-pocket cost is your deductible.

6

New Roof. Done.

Most homes completed in 1–2 days. Full cleanup, final walkthrough, and warranty documents. You’re covered for decades.

Important

Colorado Law & Your Claim

Colorado law requires homeowners to file storm damage claims within 1 year of the date of loss. Don’t wait — contact us right away so we can document everything properly.

We help homeowners fight denied or underpaid claims. We can provide additional documentation, request a re-inspection, or recommend a public adjuster if needed.

What to avoid
Door-to-door storm chasers. Out-of-state contractors who show up after a storm, take deposits, and disappear. Always verify Colorado licensing.

Anyone offering to eat your deductible. This is insurance fraud — illegal in Colorado under SB 38, and it can void your policy.

Waiting too long. Colorado law generally requires storm damage claims within 1 year of date of loss.
